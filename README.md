### About

Third-year **Data Science** undergraduate at **International University – VNU-HCM (HCMIU)** in Ho Chi Minh City, currently working as a **Business Automation & Analytics Intern** at Hebekery.

I build practical data pipelines and internal operational tools: structuring marketing and ERP data into explicit **Data Contracts**, automating scheduled syncs with **n8n** and **REST APIs** into **Lark Base**, and verifying business metrics in **SQL** and **Power BI (DAX)** before shipping dashboards or workflows.
