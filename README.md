### About

Third-year **Data Science** undergraduate at **International University – VNU-HCM (HCMIU)** in Ho Chi Minh City, currently working as a **Business Automation & Analytics Intern** at Hebekery.

I focus on turning messy operational and marketing datasets into reliable systems teams can verify and maintain:

- **Marketing & E-Commerce Analytics** — Framing business questions into KPI, Metric, and Logic Trees, locking field-level Data Contracts and grain definitions, and auditing Meta Ads performance in **Power BI (DAX)** and **SQL**.
- **Workflow & Data Automation** — Building scheduled ingestion pipelines from **Meta Ads API**, **MISA AMIS**, and operational sheets into **Lark Base** with **n8n**, **Python**, and **REST APIs**, plus supporting relational schema design for HRM and payroll modules.
- **Internal Web Tools & Agentic Workspaces** — Building context-driven personal operating systems (`Duy AI OS`) and lightweight **TypeScript / React / Three.js** interfaces on top of Lark Base operational databases.

### How I Work

| Principle | Practice |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Contract Before Dashboard** | Clarify the business question, owner, table grain, and key relationships before writing DAX or building visuals. |
| **2. Evidence Over Assumption** | Separate system-verified numbers from domain-owner rules and analytical hypotheses; verify totals against source exports. |
| **3. Human-in-the-Loop AI** | Use AI agents and MCP tooling to speed up implementation while keeping explicit human checks on input definitions, logic, and failure modes. |
